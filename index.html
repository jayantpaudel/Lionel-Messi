06 — A CAREER IN CHAPTERS

THE ROAD
TO TEN.

From a first-team debut in 2004 to a world title in 2022, these are only a few of the milestones behind the number.

  1. 1987

    Born in Rosario

    Lionel Andrés Messi is born in Santa Fe, Argentina.

  2. 2004

    Barcelona debut

    At 17, Messi makes his competitive first-team debut for FC Barcelona.

  3. 2009

    A first Ballon d’Or

    Barcelona’s historic six-trophy year ends with the first of Messi’s eight Ballon d’Or awards.

  4. 2012

    Ninety-one goals

    He finishes the calendar year with 91 goals for club and country.

  5. 2021

    South American champion

    Messi lifts the Copa América with Argentina at the Maracanã.

  6. 2022

    The final crown

    Argentina win the FIFA World Cup in Qatar; Messi receives the Golden Ball.

  7. 2023

    A new chapter in Miami

    Messi joins Inter Miami and helps the club win its first trophy, the Leagues Cup.
